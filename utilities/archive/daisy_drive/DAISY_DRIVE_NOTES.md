### Daisy Drive House Test Trivia!

For context, Daisy Drive is a Team Vanquish map in Plants vs Zombies: Battle for Neighborville (BFN). And also my favorite map in the entire game. *(It’s also a reimagining of another map, Suburban Flats, from Plants vs Zombies: Garden Warfare 1)*

I love the bright and wacky suburban theme and how open the map is. Most maps in BFN are quite closed off with very few high ground options. And even those options are tightly controlled with limited power.

**Daisy Drive is different.** *There’s roofs, perches, tree houses, and actual trees!* There’s so many options to move around the map and fight creatively.

However, because BFN is BFN, most characters can’t use the creatively designed geometry because of the lack of universal vertical mobility. This was partial inspiration for the Monkanic’s climbing ability.

Since every player can climb most surfaces, everyone can access and fight on the vertical plane. *No need for a contextual ability.*

**I, someday, want to reimagine Daisy Drive in Monkanics.** I’m not sure how I’d do it or what I’d call the map right now, but it’s definitely on my bucket list.

This sloppy house model is my first attempt at this. I wanted to replicate the slant and height of the roof, then add a twist with an interior and a climbable chimney. 

**It was very fun to work on and I hope to make this a reality in Monkanics one day.**
